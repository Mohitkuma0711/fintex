# FinTax AI: Market Comparison

**Prepared:** 8 October 2026
**Purpose:** Hackathon product positioning and roadmap discussion
**Scope:** Directional comparison of FinTax AI with common Indian individual
income-tax filing options. This is not a procurement recommendation, legal
opinion, or audited feature/pricing comparison.

## Executive summary

Indian taxpayers can use the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal, commercial
self-filing products, or assisted filing services. These options primarily help
users prepare and submit a return, with different levels of automation and human
assistance.

FinTax AI is designed around a broader, explainable workflow:
**Upload → Extract → Verify → Calculate → Suggest → Explain & Act**. Its
intended distinction is not simply “AI tax filing”: document extraction is
separate from tax arithmetic, calculations are intended to use a deterministic,
versioned rules engine, and factual explanations are intended to cite official
sources.

**Important maturity caveat:** this repository is a hackathon prototype, not a
production-ready tax service. Upload and extraction workflows, tax-engine
scaffolding, suggestion generation, and what-if simulation code exist. Tax rules
and product terms still contain TODO values, so tax calculations and suggestions
fail closed until a reviewed ruleset is configured. Cited RAG answers and
report-generation capabilities are not yet implemented. Do not represent gated
or planned capabilities as currently available.

## Directional landscape

### Official e-filing portal

The Income Tax Department portal is the authoritative destination for
government-provided e-filing services. FinTax AI should complement—not imply
replacement of—the official portal. Any future filing or “act” capability must
be checked against current portal procedures, permitted integrations, and
applicable law.

### Commercial self-filing products

Products such as ClearTax and Quicko are examples of commercial,
software-led tax-preparation options. Their plans, supported cases,
document-import capabilities, and assistance options can change. Compare the
current product and plan directly rather than assuming that a brand-level
description applies to every user.

### Assisted filing services

TaxBuddy is an example of a service positioned around digital tax assistance.
The exact level of professional review, eligibility, turnaround, and included
services should be confirmed for the specific plan and tax year.

## FinTax AI's proposed differentiation

1. **Extraction is not calculation.** Gemini may structure visible document
   content; it must not decide tax amounts. This separation reduces the risk of
   an LLM inventing a financial result.
2. **Review before relying.** The product flow makes extracted fields,
   confidence, and source-page information available for taxpayer verification
   before downstream use.
3. **Auditable arithmetic.** A deterministic engine and effective-dated rules
   are intended to make calculations repeatable and traceable to their inputs
   and rule references.
4. **Evidence-led explanations.** Planned factual answers should be grounded in
   official-source material and cite those sources, rather than present
   unreferenced conversational output as authoritative.
5. **Privacy-conscious document handling.** The prototype uses authenticated
   access and private storage paths; security, retention, deletion, and
   operational controls need further production validation.

These are design differentiators and product goals, not a claim that every
workflow is production-complete today.

## Risks and validation priorities

- **Tax correctness:** source and independently verify every rule value, effective
  date, eligibility condition, and transition before enabling calculations.
- **Tax-year coverage:** define the supported assessment/financial years and
  behavior for amended or superseded rules.
- **Extraction quality:** measure field-level accuracy on representative,
  synthetic and consented test documents; preserve confidence and provenance,
  and keep user review in the loop.
- **Security and privacy:** complete threat modeling, malware scanning, access
  controls, retention/deletion policy, incident response, and external security
  review before handling real taxpayer documents.
- **Advice boundaries:** distinguish factual information, estimates, and
  professional advice; show citations and uncertainty where appropriate.
- **Market validation:** interview target users and tax professionals to test
  whether document understanding and explainability solve a meaningful problem
  beyond existing filing journeys.
